### Version 3 — Narrative

Since 2011, I have been a software engineer at Microsoft Asia Pacific R&D, working across projects spanning traditional software, cloud computing, and open-source cloud-native. I am now a **Principal Software Engineer** on the [Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/aks/what-is-aks) team within Microsoft's Cloud + AI division, leading storage and AI infrastructure — currently focused on building **AI/LLM inference infrastructure on Kubernetes** through projects like [KAITO](https://github.com/kaito-project/kaito) and the [KEDA-KAITO Scaler](https://github.com/kaito-project/keda-kaito-scaler). As one of the earliest members of the AKS team, I built the container storage service from the ground up; it now runs in production across hundreds of thousands of Microsoft-managed clusters worldwide.

Beyond internal engineering, I am deeply involved in the global cloud-native community as a maintainer of multiple CNCF projects — including the upstream Kubernetes [NFS](https://github.com/kubernetes-csi/csi-driver-nfs) and [SMB](https://github.com/kubernetes-csi/csi-driver-smb) CSI drivers, the [sig-storage-local-static-provisioner](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/sig-storage-local-static-provisioner), the Azure CSI drivers for [Disk](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/azuredisk-csi-driver) / [File](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/azurefile-csi-driver) / [Blob](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/blob-csi-driver), and [cloud-provider-azure](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/cloud-provider-azure). I received the [**2022 Kubernetes Community Contributor Award (SIG Storage)**](https://www.kubernetes.dev/community/awards/2022/#storage), was ranked the [**#1 CNCF open-source contributor from China in 2024**](https://all.devstats.cncf.io/d/66/developer-activity-counts-by-companies?orgId=1&var-period_name=Last%20year&var-metric=commits&var-repogroup_name=All&var-country_name=China&var-companies=All), and was nominated for the **CNCF Top Committer Award (2025)**. I have spoken at KubeCon Europe and multiple KubeCon Hong Kong editions, and mentor students worldwide through the [LFX / Linux Community Bridge](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/project/cc183437-7d11-41f3-ab6f-015c63629b44) program, helping grow the next generation of cloud-native contributors.
